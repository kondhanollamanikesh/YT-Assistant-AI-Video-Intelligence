🎥 YT Assistant — AI Video Intelligence

YT Assistant is an AI-powered video intelligence platform that transforms YouTube videos into interactive, searchable knowledge bases.

Instead of simply watching a long video, users can provide a YouTube URL and interact with its content through AI-generated summaries, key insights, semantic search, quizzes, and contextual conversations.

🚀 How It Works
YouTube Video
      ↓
   Transcript
      ↓
Text Processing
      ↓
   Embeddings
      ↓
     FAISS
      ↓
   AI / RAG
      ↓
┌─────────┬──────────┬─────────┬─────────┐
│ Summary │  Search  │  Quiz   │   Chat  │
└─────────┴──────────┴─────────┴─────────┘

The system converts the video's transcript into vector embeddings and stores them in FAISS, allowing the AI to retrieve relevant parts of the video before generating responses.

✨ Features
🎬 YouTube Video Analysis — Analyze content directly from a YouTube video.
📝 AI Summarization — Generate concise summaries of lengthy videos.
💡 Key Insights — Extract important concepts and information.
🔎 Semantic Search — Search the video based on meaning rather than exact keywords.
🧠 AI Chat — Ask questions about the video's content.
📝 AI Quiz Generation — Generate questions to test understanding.
📚 Knowledge Base — Convert video content into a reusable AI knowledge source.
🎯 Grounded Responses — Retrieve relevant transcript content before generating answers.

🎯 Project Goal

The goal of YT Assistant is to transform passive video consumption into an interactive learning and knowledge experience.

Rather than asking an LLM generic questions, users can interact with an AI system that is specifically grounded in the content of the video.
